These pages cover the parts of Mythweaver that help you build and manage your stories rather than play them. Most of them are reachable from the top bar or the command palette.

## Pages in this section

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Chat with Mythweaver](assistant.md) | The built-in assistant that browses and edits your characters, lore, prompts and settings through tool calls you approve. |
| [Command palette](command-palette.md) | The Ctrl-P / ⌘-P launcher for navigation and chat actions. |
| [Import and export](import-export.md) | Bringing in SillyTavern cards, lore books, chats and presets, and exporting your own items, at `/import` and `/export`. |
| [Tags](tags.md) | Namespaced tags, siblings and parents at `/tags`, and the tag filter on every list. |
| [Jobs and analytics](jobs-and-analytics.md) | The generation queue at `/jobs` and the usage charts at `/analytics`. |
| [MCP access](mcp.md) | Connecting outside AI clients to Mythweaver's tools with an access token. |
